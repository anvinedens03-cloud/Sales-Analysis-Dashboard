## 📌 Project Title / Headline
Sales Analysis Dashboard

---

📝 Short Description / Purpose

This dashboard provides a complete analysis of dry fruit sales across regions, customer segments, and time periods.
It exists to help businesses optimize sales strategy, identify top-performing products, and improve customer satisfaction.

---

 🛠 Tech Stack

* **Power BI** → for building the dashboard, DAX measures, and interactivity
* **Excel (.xlsx)** → as the main dataset containing sales, products, and customer details
* **Power Query** → for data transformation, cleaning, and model preparation

---

📊 Data Source

* Dataset: Dry fruits sales transaction data.
* Structure:

  * Customer details (ID, region, age, gender)
  * Product info (dry fruit type, category, price)
  * Sales data (quantity sold, total revenue, date of sale)
* Usage: Used to track top customers, best-selling dry fruits, and revenue performance across regions.

---

 🌟 Features & Highlights

* Top Customers→ ranked by revenue contribution
* Best-Selling Dry Fruits → by quantity and sales value
* Revenue Trends → over time (monthly/quarterly analysis)
* Demographics Analysis → sales breakdown by region, gender, and age group
* KPIs → total sales, total revenue, average order value

---

🔎 Dashboard Explanation Format

1. Business Problem

The company needed clarity on which dry fruits drive the most revenue, who the top customers are, and how sales vary across regions and time. Lack of visibility led to missed opportunities in marketing and inventory planning.

2. Goal of the Dashboard

To build an interactive dashboard that highlights sales performance, customer contribution, and product trends, enabling data-driven decisions for sales growth and customer retention.

 3. Walkthrough of Key Visuals 

* Card KPIs → Total Revenue, Total Quantity Sold, Average Sales per Customer
* Bar Chart → Top 10 customers by sales value
* Column Chart → Top 5 dry fruits by revenue
* Line Chart → Monthly revenue trend
* Pie/Donut Chart → Regional sales distribution
* Map (if location exists) → Customer density by region

 4. Business Impact & Insights

* Identified top customers → enables loyalty programs and personalized offers.
* Highlighted best-selling dry fruits→ helps in inventory management.
* Revealed seasonal demand trends→ assists in supply chain planning.
* Showed regional contribution→ supports targeted marketing campaigns.
